# NRIClub outreach kit

Ready-to-send messages for getting the first members in each city. Copy, change the parts in `[brackets]`, send.

Links to use:
- Home: `https://nriclub.app`
- A city page: `https://nriclub.app/city/[city]`, in lower case with hyphens for spaces (for example `https://nriclub.app/city/berlin` or `https://nriclub.app/city/new-york-city`). It shows the city's member, event and desi-place counts, works without an account, and has its own link preview ("Indians in Berlin · NRIClub").
- The guides: `https://nriclub.app/guides`, ten short guides anyone can read (passport renewal, OCI, NRE/NRO accounts, taxes, sending money home and more). Good to share when someone asks one of these questions in a WhatsApp group, for example `https://nriclub.app/guides/oci-card-the-basics`.
- Your invite link: in NRIClub, open the ⚙ menu, then **Invite friends**. People who join through it name you as their voucher, so they're verified faster.

Shared links show a preview card with the NRIClub logo (a saffron N on a dark square) and "One home for Indians abroad".

## 1. WhatsApp and Telegram group admins

Most cities already have Indian WhatsApp or Telegram groups. Ask the admin first; don't post without asking.

**First message to the admin (private):**

> Hi [Name], I'm [your name], I live in [City] too. Thanks for running [group name], it's really useful.
>
> I'm helping build NRIClub (nriclub.app), a free site just for Indians abroad. Our team checks every new member, and each city has its own group, desi places list, events and guides (passport, OCI, NRE/NRO, taxes).
>
> It isn't meant to replace your group. It's for the things WhatsApp is bad at: finding old answers, housing and referrals, and events. Would you be OK with me sharing it once in the group? Happy to make you the [City] ambassador if you'd like.

**Post for the group (after the admin agrees):**

> For everyone in [City]: NRIClub is a free community for Indians abroad, and our team checks every new member.
>
> • Ask questions and find answers later (no scrolling through 1,000 messages)
> • Rooms, flatmates and job referrals
> • Desi groceries, restaurants and temples, recommended by members
> • Diwali, Holi and meetups near you
>
> [City] page: https://nriclub.app/city/[city]

## 2. Indian Student Associations

Students arrive every semester and need the most help. Reach them before the semester starts (July–August and December–January).

**Email to the association's committee:**

> Subject: Free help for new Indian students arriving in [City]
>
> Hi [Name],
>
> I'm [your name] from NRIClub (nriclub.app), a free community for Indians living abroad, where our team checks every new member.
>
> New students ask the same questions every year: where to stay, how to open a bank account, where to buy Indian groceries, how the NRE/NRO accounts work. On NRIClub they can ask people who already live in [City], and read short guides on the basics.
>
> Could you share our [City] page with your new students, in your welcome email or group? https://nriclub.app/city/[city]
>
> If someone on your committee would like to be our [City] ambassador, we'd love that too.
>
> Thank you,
> [your name]

## 5. City ambassadors

An ambassador is one member per city who welcomes newcomers and gets the group going. It takes an hour or two a month.

**What they do:**
- Say hello to new members in the city group.
- Answer or pass on questions in the group.
- Host or share one meetup or festival event a month.
- Add a few desi places (groceries, restaurants, temples).
- Share the city page with local WhatsApp groups and associations, using the messages above.

**What they get:**
- An "Ambassador · [City]" badge on their profile and in Find.
- Being the person every Indian in the city knows.

**How to make someone an ambassador:** in the admin panel, open **Members**, click the member, and under **Ambassador** click **Make ambassador**, enter the city and click **Save**. Leave the city empty and save to remove it.

People ask to become one through **Become a city ambassador** on the home page and on every city page (it emails hello@nriclub.app).

**Reply to someone who asks:**

> Hi [Name], thank you for offering to be our [City] ambassador! 🙏
>
> Here's all it is: say hello to new members in the Indians in [City] group, answer or pass on questions, and host or share one meetup a month. You'll get the Ambassador badge on your profile.
>
> If that sounds good, reply "yes" and I'll switch it on today.
