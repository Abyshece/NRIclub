# NRIClub outreach guide

How to find the first members and city ambassadors, and stay within the
law. Read section 1 before you contact anyone; then copy, change the parts
in `[brackets]`, and use.

Links to use:
- Home: `https://nriclub.app`
- A city page: `https://nriclub.app/city/[city]`, in lower case with hyphens for spaces (for example `https://nriclub.app/city/berlin` or `https://nriclub.app/city/new-york-city`). It works without an account, shows the city's group, events and desi places, and has its own link preview ("Indians in Berlin · NRIClub"). Its **Become a city ambassador** button opens the application form (also on the home page).
- The guides: `https://nriclub.app/guides`, short guides anyone can read (passport renewal, OCI, NRE/NRO accounts, taxes, sending money home and more), for example `https://nriclub.app/guides/oci-card-the-basics`.
- What's on: `https://nriclub.app/whats-on`, Indian events in Germany by other organisers.
- News: `https://nriclub.app/news`, our own short news for Indians in Germany (visas, citizenship, taxes, the embassy and consulates), each post with its official source. In the admin panel, News → **Copy for WhatsApp** gives a post's title, summary and a link that Analytics counts (campaign "news").
- Your invite link: in NRIClub, open the ⚙ menu, then **Invite friends**. Give it to people who asked you about NRIClub or said yes when you met.

No UK cities while the UK is paused (`compliance/uk-pause.md`).

## 1. The rule: no cold messages

Inviting someone to join NRIClub, to share it or to become an ambassador is
advertising, even though NRIClub is free. In Germany, advertising by
electronic message needs the person's **prior, express consent** (§ 7(2)
No. 2 UWG). That covers email, WhatsApp, Telegram, SMS and direct messages
on LinkedIn, Instagram or Facebook, and it applies to businesses and
associations too, not only to private people. A message that only asks for
permission to send information about NRIClub counts as well.

So, without consent given first:

- **Don't** write privately to WhatsApp or Telegram group admins you don't know.
- **Don't** email or message student associations, temples, gurdwaras or cultural associations.
- **Don't** message students, or people you find in a group, a member list or on a website.
- **Don't** post in someone else's group unless its admin agreed first (in person, or in a conversation they started).

A phone number or email address that is public, in a group or on a website
is not consent. Breaking the rule can bring a warning letter (Abmahnung)
with costs, and a complaint to the data protection authority.

## 2. What you can do

1. **Post on NRIClub's own channels.** NRIClub's own website, LinkedIn,
   Instagram or Facebook page. People who are interested come to us (posts
   in section 4).
2. **Talk to people in person** at festivals, meetups, university welcome
   days or temple events (script in section 5). If someone wants to hear
   more, ask whether you may write to them, and how; then note it
   (section 3).
3. **Reply when someone writes first.** A group admin, an association or a
   student who contacts NRIClub (or you about NRIClub) can get an answer
   about it, about what they asked. Don't add them to a mailing list.
4. **Introductions where a mutual contact asks first.** A friend asks the
   person whether they'd like to hear from you. Write only once the person
   said yes, and say in your first message who introduced you (section 3).
5. **Phone calls to associations and businesses** (not to private people
   or students), where you have a concrete reason to think they're
   interested: for example the public number of an Indian association to
   ask about listing its Diwali celebration on What's on. § 7(2) No. 1 UWG
   requires express consent for calls to consumers only; for others, an
   interest you can reasonably presume is enough. If they say no, don't
   call again.
6. **Notice boards and posters** where the place agreed (ask in person).
7. **A news post in a group, with the admin's OK.** Where a group's admin
   agreed first (as above), a useful news post (a rule that changed, a
   consulate notice) is the best thing to share: it helps on its own, and
   the link leads to NRIClub. Once per post, no follow-ups, and stop if the
   admin or the group asks you to.

## 3. Keep a note, and say where the details came from

**Note each consent.** For every person or organisation you may write to:
who, how to reach them, when and how they agreed (in person at [event], they
wrote first, introduced by [name]), and what to (for example "OK to email
about the ambassador role"). Keep the list private, only for this, and stop
writing to anyone who says no. Delete their entry when you no longer write
to them, unless you need it to show the consent.

**First contact (Art. 14 GDPR).** When you write to someone whose details
you didn't get from them (an introduction, an association's website), say
in the first message who you are, where you got their details, why you're
writing, and that you'll delete them if they ask. Link to the Privacy
Policy (`https://nriclub.app/privacy`). For example:

> Hi [Name], I'm [your name] from NRIClub (nriclub.app). [Mutual contact] gave me your number and said you'd be happy to hear about [what]. If you'd rather not, just tell me and I'll delete your details. How we handle personal data: https://nriclub.app/privacy

## 5. In person

**Talking points** (all true today; don't promise more):

- Free, for Indians and people of Indian origin abroad.
- Our team checks every new member; your phone number stays private.
- One group for each city. Questions and answers stay findable instead of scrolling away in a chat.
- Rooms and flatmates (the housing search, with "Looking for a place"), job referrals, a travel board for finding a companion on a flight.
- Desi groceries, restaurants and temples, recommended by members.
- Events and meetups; in Germany also What's on, with other organisers' events.
- Short guides on passports, OCI, NRE/NRO accounts, taxes and sending money home.
- It doesn't replace your WhatsApp group: it's for what a chat is bad at.

**A short script:**

> Hi, I'm [your name]. I help run NRIClub, a free site for Indians abroad. Each city has its own group, and our team checks every new member, so it's a bit like your WhatsApp group, but answers don't get lost and nobody sees your phone number. Are you in [City]? Here's our page [show the city page or the QR code]. Would you like me to send you the link? (If yes: "By email or WhatsApp?" — note it.)

**Asking someone to be an ambassador** (for example a group admin you meet):

> You clearly know everyone here. Would you like to be NRIClub's ambassador for [City]? It's voluntary: welcome new members in the city group, answer what you can, and host a meetup now and then, an hour or two a month. You'd get the ambassador badge. You can apply on our city page under "Become a city ambassador"; or, if you like, I'll send you the link. (Note the yes.)

## 6. City ambassadors

An ambassador is a member who welcomes newcomers in their city and gets the
group going. They accept the ambassador agreement in their panel
(`src/content/ambassador.js`) and follow its guidelines: they contact
members only on NRIClub, and they never remove or judge content (they flag
it to our team). Ambassadors who spread the word follow this guide too: no
cold messages, also not to other groups' admins.

**What they do:**
- Welcome new members of the city group (one message in the panel).
- Answer or pass on questions in the group.
- Host or share a meetup or festival event now and then.
- Add a few desi places (groceries, restaurants, temples).
- Tell people about NRIClub in person and on their own public posts.

**What they get:**
- An "Ambassador · [City]" badge on their profile.
- Being the person newcomers in the city know.

**Applications** come in through **Become a city ambassador** on the home
page and on every city page, and show in the admin panel under
**Ambassadors**, where the reply email is written. To make a member an
ambassador: admin panel → **Members** → the member → **Ambassador** → enter
the city → **Save** (empty and save to remove it).
